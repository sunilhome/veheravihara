This temple, located in the village of Niwanthidiya in the Colombo district of the Western Province, was established in 1948.
The original incumbent was Niwanthidiya Sri Saddhananda Nayaka Thero. After that, Madapatha Dhammaloka Nayaka Thero served as the incumbent. The current incumbent is Sastrapathi Seenitellae Chandarama Nayaka Thero.
Dhamma classes are held here on both Saturdays and Sundays. Small classes are held on Saturdays from 1 to 5 and on Sundays from 6 to 10 p.m.
The temple is home to the Community Welfare Society,
the Temple Task Force Society, the Temple Task Force Youth Society and the Sri Saddhananda Dhamma School Alumni Association.
A valuable shrine.
