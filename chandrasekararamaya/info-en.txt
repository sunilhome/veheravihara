The Chandrasekararamaya Temple is located on Ananda Maithri Mawatha in Maharagama village, Colombo district, Western Province.
This temple was founded by Ananda Maithri Thero in 1972. After that, Denihene Ananda Murthi Thero served as the incumbent. The current incumbent, Molamure Nandarama Thero, arrived here in 1976.
Thero is the Chief Sangha Nayaka of the Amarapura Nikaya in the Western Province, Deputy Parivenadhipathi of the Delgoda Kalyana Pradeepa Pirivena and Justice of the Peace (for the entire island). He also holds the post of Headmaster of the Dhamma School of the temple. The children of the Dhamma School observe the Sil Samadan on the last Sunday of the month. His birthday is on 18 December 1966. Currently, he is serving as a teacher at the Delgoda Kalyana Pradeepa Vidyayathana Pirivena.
The Venerable Thero performs auspicious rituals such as protecting houses from evil spirits and evil spirits with the power of Buddha. It is a temple that provides valuable service to the area.
