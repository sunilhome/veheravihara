The Chandrasekararamaya Temple is located on Ananda Maithri Mawatha in Maharagama Village, Colombo District, Western Province.
This temple was founded by Sri Sumangala Thero in 1972. The current incumbent, Molamure Nandarama Thero, arrived here in 1979.
The Chief Sangha Nayaka of the Amarapura Nikaya in the Western Province, Venerable Denihene Ananda Murthi Thero, is the Deputy Parivenadhipathi of the Delgoda Kalyana Pradeepa Pirivena,
 Justice of the Peace (for the entire island), Headmaster of the Dhamma School of the temple. The children of the Dhamma School observe the Sil Samadan on the last Sunday of the month. He currently serves as a teacher at the Delgoda Kalyana Pradeepa Vidyayathana Pirivena.
He performs peaceful rituals such as protecting houses from evil spirits with the power of Buddha's virtues.
