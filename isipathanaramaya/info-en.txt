The Sangha lineage belonging to the Nawinna Isipathanarama Viharaya is:
 Venerable Saralankara Sangharaja Thero, 
 Venerable Ginigat Pitiye Sangharakkhita Thero, 
 Venerable Dhammakitthi Thero, 
 Venerable Gangodawila Piyadassi Thero (Incumbent of Gangowila Sri Vijayarama Rajamaha Viharaya) 
 Venerable Bogoda Dhamma Kitthi Thero (Former Incumbent of Kottawa Siri Dhamma Kitthikarama) 
 Venerable Palagama Gnanajothi Thero, 
 Venerable Hokandara Dhammananda Thero, 
 Venerable Hokandara Wimala Thero, 
 Venerable Maharagama Nanda Thero. 
 
The founder of this Nawinna Isipathanaramaya temple is Venerable Hokandara Dhammananda Thero
purchased this place from his uncle in 1906. 
In 2022, offerings were made to the Maha Sangha.
